LivingLink Design System

Light, refined, never over-designed. One ink, one paper, one accent, one marker — the product's own highlight is the brand's only flourish.

Logo

The Anchored Comment — a bubble standing on a marker stroke, with the iris dot nested in a punched-out notch. Canonical files in assets/brand/: logo.svg, logo-mark.svg, logo-inverse.svg, logo-mono.svg, app-icon.svg. Final wordmark wants a geometric grotesk (Inter Tight / General Sans); system stack used for the spec.

Primary lockup

Default for all light surfaces. The notch is part of the mark — it ships in the file, never redrawn.

On dark

White ink, accents hold.

One color

Print, emboss, single-ink contexts.

Mark alone

Below 120px width, or as avatar/favicon.

Clearspace

x = the marker stroke height. Keep at least 1x of clear space on all sides; nothing enters the box.

Minimum sizes

Below these, the notch closes up and the mark loses its read.

DecisionWhy
Bottom-center floating dockEstablished affordance (media players, iOS tab bars, Figma toolbar). One place to look, thumb-reachable, stays out of the reading column.
One slot, two states"Comments · n" and "Hide" share a slot — open/close is one mental control, and the unresolved count is always visible.
Status chips above the dock"Elsewhere" and "orphaned" are ambient status, not actions — they hover near the controls but can't be clicked by accident.
Glass kept to one surfaceOnly the dock blurs. Liquid-glass everywhere reads heavy; one frosted pill reads considered.
